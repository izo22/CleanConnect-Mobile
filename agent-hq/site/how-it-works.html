How Orvanta works

Your automations do the work. You keep the final say.

Orvanta is the control room for your AI agents and your n8n or Zapier automations. You see what they do, you can stop them, and they ask your permission before the actions that matter.

The problem

An AI agent doesn't wait for anyone

Your automations send quotes, answer customers, issue refunds and chase invoices on their own. When the AI gets a price wrong, makes a promise you never would, or gets stuck in a loop, you often find out from the customer. Orvanta sits right before the sensitive actions, so nothing important goes out without your OK.

What Orvanta does
SeeEach workflow or Zap becomes an "agent". Its successes, failures and incidents show up, explained in plain English.
StopOne Pause button per agent. For n8n, the workflow is really switched off, in under a second.
ControlBefore a sensitive action, the agent asks for permission. Depending on your rules, it goes through, or it waits for you.
A real example

An $18,400 quote that waits for your OK

A solar installer automated its quotes: a form comes in, the AI sizes the system and writes the quote, the quote goes to the customer. They protected the "Send the quote" step and set one rule: over $10,000, I approve.

Your settings

You decide when you're asked

Protected steps

You choose where the agent stops to ask: sending an email, a payment, a post. Every other step runs as usual.

Each agent's freedom

  • I approve everything: every request waits for you, even for $10. Best at the start.
  • My rules decide: what respects your guardrails goes through on its own; the rest waits for you.
GuardrailWhat it prevents

AI checkAn answer that's wrong, made up, or contradicts what the customer asked

Connecting your tools

With the tools you already use

The simplest

n8n

  1. You create an API key in n8n and paste it into Orvanta.
  2. Each workflow shows up as an agent.
  3. On the agent's page, "Ask me before a step": Orvanta adds the check to the workflow itself. Nothing to do in n8n.
One step to add

Zapier

  1. You accept the invitation to the Orvanta app and paste your Zapier key.
  2. In the Zap, you add the "Ask Permission" step right before the sensitive action.
  3. The Zap waits for your click in Decisions, then carries on by itself.

Zapier doesn't let outside tools edit your Zaps, so this step is added once, by hand. Multi-step Zaps need a paid Zapier plan.

Security

When in doubt, nothing goes out

We never ask for a password

You create the keys yourself, on your own screen, and paste them into your own Orvanta account. Nobody at Orvanta needs your n8n or Zapier password.

Limited access, easy to cut

The n8n key only lets Orvanta read your workflows, pause them and add its check; it can't delete anything. Orvanta can't read or edit your Zaps. Delete the key or disconnect in one click, any time.

If Orvanta doesn't answer

The protected step doesn't run. An outage blocks the action instead of letting it through unchecked.

Your workspace is yours

Each customer has a separate workspace. Even our administrators don't see your agents or data unless you invite them. Hosted in the US, backed up daily.

Questions
Can I edit a quote before approving it?

No, it's yes or no. If something is wrong, decline it and fix it yourself. The agent sends nothing.

What if I don't answer?

The action waits: up to 24 hours with n8n, then it's dropped; up to 30 days with Zapier. Nothing ever goes out without your OK.

Who can approve?

The workspace owner and members. You can invite a colleague as a viewer: they see everything but can't decide.

Does it slow my automations down?

No. When an action respects your rules, Orvanta answers in a fraction of a second. Only the actions that need your OK wait.

Does an AI read my data?

Only if you turn on the AI check or write your own rules: an AI then re-reads the message before it goes out. You can turn it off in Guardrails.

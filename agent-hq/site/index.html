How it works

See. Stop. Control.

Three levels, from watching your agents to deciding what they're allowed to do. Each agent's page shows exactly which levels apply.

See

Every agent gets a profile: mission, owner, budget, trust level. Failures, cost spikes and quality issues become plain-English alerts that say what's happening, why, and the impact.

Stop

Pause any agent in one click. For n8n workflows, Orvanta really switches the workflow off, and switches it off again if someone turns it back on.

Control

Agents ask before sensitive actions. Under your thresholds they go ahead. Above them, they wait for your Approve, and the workflow resumes the moment you decide.

Alerts

Alerts that tell you what to do.

  • Repeated actions, expired access, wrong language, cost spikes, budgets running out
  • One-click fixes: pause, roll back the instructions, hand over to a human, switch to a cheaper model
  • Alerts close themselves once the problem is gone
Your AI team

Every agent gets a profile.

  • Mission, owner, budget and trust level, like any team member
  • Success rate, tasks and time saved over the last 24 hours
  • A clear statement of what Orvanta can and can't do with it
Decisions

Approvals before anything risky.

  • Refunds, quotes or vouchers above your threshold wait for a human
  • Approve and the action runs; decline and nothing happens
  • Rules written in plain English: “Human approval for any refund over $200”
Everything in one place

Built for the person who's accountable.

Plain-English guardrails

Amount thresholds, quiet hours, a monthly AI budget, automatic pause when an agent loops.

AI review of answers

A share of each agent's answers is re-read and checked against its instructions. Made-up facts get flagged; you decide.

Report a mistake

Anyone on the team can flag a bad answer. The owner is alerted and it shows up in the alerts.

Budgets and costs

Monthly budget per agent, spend per task, and a warning before you blow through the cap.

Trust levels

Level 0: a human approves everything. Level 1: sensitive actions only. Level 2: autonomous, guardrails still apply.

Versioned instructions

Full audit log

Live, on any screen

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to rebuild my agents?

No. Connect n8n with an API key, add an Orvanta step to your Zaps, or add a few lines of code. Your agents keep running where they are.

What can Orvanta actually stop?

Agents connected through the n8n integration, the Zapier app or the API check with Orvanta before acting, so pauses and guardrails apply to them. Agents that only report their activity are monitored, not stopped, and Orvanta shows you which is which.

What happens if Orvanta is unreachable?

Sensitive steps fail closed: the workflow stops instead of running the action. When in doubt, nothing gets paid or sent.

How long does setup take?

About 5 minutes to connect n8n, and about 2 minutes per sensitive workflow to add approvals.

Do I need to be technical?

No. The dashboard is written for business owners and operations leads. Whoever built your agents connects them once.

How much does it cost?

Orvanta is free during the beta. We'll announce pricing well before it applies.

Where is my data?

On servers in the US, with daily backups. The AI review of answers is optional and can be turned off.
